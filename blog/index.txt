- Seedless Torrents [2]
  An idea I had for about 20 years: generate the file pieces from a seed
  so a torrent needs no peers. Prototyped with AI over several months,
  tried AES-CTR, Feistel networks, Merkle trees and Gray code. It failed
  past 8 bits. The seed was always larger than the data.
